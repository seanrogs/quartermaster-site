Your armies, in order.

Quartermaster is the army list manager for tabletop miniature games that knows how to play nice. Draft a force whenever inspiration strikes, and have every unit ready when it’s time to play.

Download on the App Store Read the User Guide

For iPhone and iPad. Also runs on Apple silicon Macs.

Built by wargamers, for wargamers

Every game system and faction template is made and shared by the Quartermaster community. If something’s missing, you can fix it yourself, right in the app.

🎲

Community templates

Browse, install and update game systems and factions without leaving the app.

📇

Draft with cards

Every unit is a card with profiles, options and rules. Collapse it down to just the essentials.

✅

Check your list

Points breakdowns, role percentages, targets and highlighted errors. Quartermaster never gets in your way.

🏆

Track your games

Conditions, victory points and experience for one-off games and long campaigns.

🖨️

Publish and share

Roster table and card PDFs, text summaries for events, and draft files for your friends.

🛠️

Make your own

Configure any faction or create a whole new game system, then share it with the community.
