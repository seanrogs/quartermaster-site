Play nice.

Quartermaster is the best army list manager for tabletop miniature games. Draft a force whenever inspiration strikes, and have everything ready when it’s time to play.

{% include app-store-badge.html %} Read the User Guide

For iPhone and iPad.

Built by wargamers, for wargamers

Every game system and faction template is made and shared by the Quartermaster community. If something’s missing, you can fix it yourself, right in the app.

📇

Focus on the essentials

Quartermaster doesn't try to replace your rulebooks. It focuses on glanceable in-game reminders where you need them.

✅

Draft your way

Quartermaster never gets in your way, but provides guidance when you need it. Publish for events, or share with your friends.

🏆

Celebrate victories

Manage conditions, victory points and experience, both for one-off games and long campaigns.

🛠️

Full control

Configure any faction or create a whole new game system, all right in the app. Share your work with the enthusiastic community.

Games in the catalogue

A growing list of community-made templates, including:

  • Warhammer: The Old World
  • Horus Heresy (all editions)
  • Necromunda
  • Warhammer Fantasy Battles
  • Warhammer 40,000 (many editions)
  • Trench Crusade
  • Mordheim
  • and more…
