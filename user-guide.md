## How Quartermaster works {#overview}

<ol class="start-steps" markdown="1">
<li markdown="1">**Install a game system and a faction.**
Quartermaster uses game system files and faction template files to provide all the options for your army. Every template is made by Quartermaster users and shared with the community. If something is missing, you can fix it yourself.
</li>
<li markdown="1">**Draft your force.**
Create a draft for a faction, choose its traits, add units, then set their size, options and items. Quartermaster is a *permissive* army manager: it shows you totals, limits and errors, but it never stops you building the list you want.
</li>
<li markdown="1">**Play, publish and share.**
Track Conditions, victory points and experience during games. Publish a PDF or text summary for events, or share the draft file with other Quartermaster users.
</li>
<li markdown="1">**Make it your own.**
Configure any game system or faction right in the app, from fixing a points cost to building a whole new game.
</li>
</ol>

## Install game systems and factions {#install-a-game-system-or-faction-template}

1. In the Library, tap **Browse Game Systems**. Once you have a game system installed, the button becomes **Browse Factions**.
2. Find the game system or faction you want and tap **Install**.
3. You need both a game system and at least one of its factions before you can create a draft.

The same browser shows when an installed template has an update. Tap the button next to it to install the latest version.

> Templates are made and maintained by the community. Have one to contribute? See [Share and contribute](#share-a-faction).

## Install from the Templates website {#download-a-game-system-or-faction-template}

Some older or less popular templates are only on the [Quartermaster Templates]({{ site.templates_url }}) website. Some of them are no longer maintained.

1. Open [Quartermaster Templates]({{ site.templates_url }}) in Safari.
2. Tap the game system or faction you want, then confirm the download. Remember you need both a game system and a faction.
3. Tap the downloads button in Safari, then tap the file. Quartermaster opens and imports it.
4. After the file is imported, you can delete it from Safari's Downloads list. That way, a later update to the same faction won't get renamed.

You can also open a Quartermaster game system, faction or draft file from Files, Mail, Messages or AirDrop. Quartermaster imports it.

## Find your way around {#find-your-way-around}

The **Library** is the starting view. It shows:

- **Recent Drafts** and **Recent Factions**, to jump straight back to what you were working on. On iPad, Recent Drafts is in the sidebar.
- **Game Systems**: expand a game system to see its factions, or tap its name to open it. Tap a faction to see its drafts.
- A **search field**, which searches across game systems, factions and draft names.

Tap the **Quartermaster** button in the top-left corner for this guide, to contact support, review the app, or see what's new.

On iPad you can open Quartermaster in more than one window. For example, you can work on two drafts side by side.

## Create a draft {#create-a-new-draft}

1. In the Library, open the game system, then tap the faction.
2. Tap **New Draft**.
3. The new draft opens straight away, named after the faction. To give it a better name, see [Rename](#draft-menu).
4. If the faction has [traits](#traits), such as a Legion or an allegiance, choose them at the top of the draft.

If a faction has traits and more than one draft, use **Sort by** in the Faction view to group its drafts by a trait.

## Rename, duplicate and delete {#draft-menu}

Tap the **draft name** at the top of the Draft view to open the draft menu:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Edit the draft's name in place. |
| **Share Draft** | Send the draft file to another Quartermaster user. |
| **Add Notes** | Add notes to the draft. |
| **Theme…** | Give the draft its own colours. See [Themes](#themes). |
| **Edit and Rearrange…** | Reorder or remove units and detachments. |
| **Duplicate Draft** | Make a copy and open it. Great for tracking a campaign over time, or trying a variation. |
| **Delete Draft** | Delete the draft, after asking you to confirm. |

You can also work with drafts in the Faction view:

- Swipe **right** on a draft to **Duplicate** or **Rename** it.
- Swipe **left** on a draft to delete it, or tap **Edit**, then tap the delete button.

## Add notes to a draft {#add-notes-to-a-draft}

Tap the draft name, then **Add Notes**. Draft notes are included in the draft's summary. To add notes to a single unit, see [Aliases, custom equipment and notes](#personalise-a-unit).

## Undo changes {#undo}

Most edits can be undone, including adding, deleting and duplicating units and drafts.

- **iPhone:** shake your iPhone to undo or redo.
- **iPad:** use the **Edit** menu, or press <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> on a keyboard (<kbd>⇧⌘Z</kbd> to redo).

## Add units {#enlist-units-to-a-draft}

1. In the Draft view, tap the **+** button.
2. Find the units you want:
   - Type in the search field to filter by name, or by the requirements shown under a unit's name.
   - Type a **number** to show only units that cost that much or less. Useful for spending the last few points in a draft.
3. Tap a unit to add it to the draft straight away.

To add several units at once, tap the **Select multiple units** button at the top. Use each unit's stepper to choose how many, then tap the confirm button.

Some units have sub-units, like a dedicated transport. Expand them in the list to add the sub-unit at the same time.

## Other factions and roles {#add-units-from-another-faction}

At the top of the Add list, tap the **faction name** to open more settings:

- **Faction:** choose another faction to add its units to this draft, such as allies or mercenaries.
- **To:** choose where the units go.
  - The draft's name (the default) adds units to their usual roles.
  - **Separate Detachment** keeps units from another faction in their own detachment, managed separately from the primary list. If you add another faction's units without choosing this, Quartermaster asks whether to manage them separately or combine them with the primary list.
  - Under **Custom Role**, pick a different role than usual. For example, use this when an army book moves a unit from Rare to Special.

If the draft has [traits](#traits) chosen, the Add list only shows units that suit them.

## Duplicate, move and rearrange {#manage-units}

- **Duplicate:** swipe right on a unit, or touch and hold it and choose **Duplicate**. The copy is added to the same role.
- **Delete:** swipe left on a unit, or touch and hold it and choose **Delete**. Changed your mind? [Undo](#undo).
- **Rearrange:** drag a unit card to a new position in its role. Or tap the draft name, choose **Edit and Rearrange…**, and drag units using the handles.
- **Change Role:** touch and hold a unit and choose **Change Role** to move it to another role in the draft.
- **Change Detachment:** for a unit inside a detachment, touch and hold it and choose **Change Detachment** to move it to the same role in another detachment.

You can also do all of these from inside a unit: tap the **unit's name** at the top for Duplicate, Change Role or Change Detachment, and Delete.

Tap the **Collapse details** button in the toolbar to hide profiles and special rules, so you see just the essentials of each unit.

## Set a unit's size {#set-a-units-size}

Tap a unit to open it. Then:

- Tap the size field in the middle and type a size. Sizes outside the unit's minimum and maximum are adjusted to fit.
- Tap the step buttons on either side to change the size by a set amount, like **−1**, **+1** or **+5**.
- Touch and hold a step button to choose a different step, or to add or subtract any amount.
- Tap **min** or **max** to jump to the unit's minimum or maximum size.

## Options and upgrades {#equip-unit-options-and-upgrades}

Each option the unit can take is listed with its cost.

- Tap an option to equip it. Equipped options are highlighted with a checkmark, or a count if they can be taken more than once.
- Keep tapping to equip an option again. Once you reach the maximum, the next tap resets the count.
- Swipe **right** on an option to equip the **Maximum** number.
- Swipe **left** on an option to **Remove 1** or **Clear** it, or touch and hold it and choose **Clear**.
- Some options have their own upgrades. After you equip one, tap the chevron to see and equip its upgrades.
- Options that change the unit's characteristics update its profile. Changed characteristics are shown in **bold**.

Some units have **points multipliers**, for example a percentage cost increase for a special rule. These appear as their own section. Tap one to apply it.

When the draft has [traits](#traits) chosen, options and armoury items meant for other trait variants are hidden. Anything already equipped stays visible, so you can still remove it.

## Armoury items {#equip-special-items}

Some units can choose items from a shared pool. Examples are magic items in Warhammer: The Old World, skills in Necromunda, or upgrade cards in X-Wing.

1. In the unit view, tap an armoury category, such as **Magic Items**. Units with several categories show a summary of what's equipped in each one.
2. Tap an item to add it. Tap again to add another, if the item allows it.
3. To remove items, tap the **x** next to them, or use **Clear**.

To narrow the list:

- Tap **Filter**, then **Filter to remaining points**, to show only items you can still afford. **Filter to faction items only** hides the generic items.
- Type in the search field to filter by name, or type a number to show items up to that cost.

Some items change the unit's profile too. Adjusted characteristics are shown in bold.

## Attach a sub-unit {#attach-a-sub-unit}

Some units can have sub-units attached. Examples are a dedicated transport, a mount, a unit that can only be taken with another unit, or the individual models in a complicated unit.

1. Open the unit.
2. Tap **Attach** (the paperclip button) in the bottom toolbar, and choose the sub-units to add. Touch and hold **Attach** for a quick menu of each sub-unit.

Attached units appear inside the parent unit. Tap one to configure it.

## Aliases, custom equipment and notes {#personalise-a-unit}

You can personalise a unit without changing the faction. Open the unit, then tap the **unit's name** at the top:

- **Rename** gives the unit an alias, like your character's name. The original unit name is still shown underneath. Use **Clear Alias** to remove it.
- **Add Custom Equipment** adds a custom option, which is good for house rules, campaign rewards or rules from a supplement. Swipe right on the new entry and tap **Edit** to set its name, cost, rules and profile. To change the unit's characteristics, put the change in the profile field, like `Ld+1`. Tap the entry to equip it.
- **Add Notes** adds notes to this unit.

## Traits {#traits}

Traits are army-wide choices, like a Legion, a sub-faction or an allegiance. Each trait has **variants** to choose from. For example, a Legion trait might offer Blood Angels, Imperial Fists or Raven Guard.

If the faction or game system has traits, they appear as buttons at the top of the draft. Tap one to choose a variant. Some traits let you choose several variants, up to a limit, and some variants show requirements as a reminder.

Your choices shape the rest of the draft:

- **Add list:** only units that suit your variants are offered. Units for other variants are left out.
- **Options and items:** upgrades and armoury items for other variants are hidden.
- **Special rules:** a trait name in brackets, like `[Legiones Astartes]`, is replaced by the variant you chose, like "Raven Guard". This shows on unit cards, the unit view, lookups, summaries and PDFs.
- **Profiles:** a variant can adjust the characteristics of units that carry its trait, like M+1.
- **Limits:** a variant can change composition rules, like how much of the army each unit in a role may be, or how many units of the same name a role may have.
- **Colours:** a variant can have its own [theme](#themes), which tints the draft.

**Detachments** have their own trait buttons. They follow the draft's choice, shown as "same as" the draft, until you choose something different for that detachment. Units inside a detachment follow the detachment's choice.

Your chosen variants are shown on detachment cards, in summaries and in published PDFs.

## Links {#links}

Some units and detachments must be paired with something else in the draft. For example, a unit might have to be assigned to a particular character, or a detachment might need a commander. Quartermaster shows a **Link** button above the card of anything that needs a link.

- **Make a link:** tap **Link** and choose a unit or detachment from the list. Only the ones that qualify are offered.
- **Follow a link:** a linked card shows what it's linked to. Tap it and choose **View** to jump to the linked unit or detachment, even in another detachment. The linked card is briefly highlighted.
- **Remove a link:** tap the link and choose **Remove Link**.
- **Automatic links:** when a newly added unit or detachment has only one possible match, it's linked straight away.

The card being linked to shows what links to it. When [Highlight errors](#highlight-errors) is on, a missing link is shown in red. Links are hidden while details are collapsed, and they're included in published PDFs.

## Themes {#themes}

Give each draft its own colours. Tap the draft name, then **Theme…**:

1. Turn on **Theme**.
2. Choose a **base** colour and a **trim** colour. Quartermaster warns you if the trim may be hard to read on the base.
3. Turn on **Shade** to always show the draft in Dark Mode with the theme.

A theme tints the draft's cards and Add button, and the draft's row in Recent Drafts. If a [trait](#traits) variant has its own colours, the draft uses those automatically, and your own theme replaces them. Changed your mind while editing? Tap **Undo** in the theme editor, or shake your iPhone.

## Points breakdown {#points-breakdown}

The draft's total is shown in the top-right corner. Tap it to see:

- A breakdown of points by role, and by detachment if you use them. Tap a total to expand it.
- Any **tallies** the faction uses, like Power Rating or model counts. Turn on **Show tallies** to show each unit's tallies on its card. See [Tallies](#work-with-tallies).

The **Breakdown** button in the toolbar has the settings for errors and percentages, described below.

## Highlight errors {#highlight-errors}

Tap **Breakdown › Highlight errors**. Quartermaster then highlights anything that breaks the faction's rules:

- a role outside its limits, like too few Core units, or more than the allowed percentage of Characters
- too many of a group of units, like more than 0–3 of two units per 1,000 points
- roles out of balance with each other, like more Elites than Troops
- a unit or detachment worth more than its share of the points, like a character over 25% of the army
- too many units of the same name, when a trait sets a limit
- a [link](#links) that's missing
- the draft total, when it's over the [target](#percentages)

While errors are highlighted, the **Breakdown** button shows how many there are. Tap **Breakdown › Errors** to see the whole list in one place, grouped by detachment. Highlighted points and role headers open a menu that explains the limit.

Highlighting is a guide only. Quartermaster never stops you from building a draft.

## Percentages and targets {#percentages}

Many games limit how much of an army can be spent on each role. Tap **Breakdown › Show percentages** to show each role's share next to its points.

Then choose what the percentages are calculated against:

- **Use current total:** the draft's exact total.
- **Round to next 10, 50 or 100:** the total rounded up. For example, a 1,980 point draft is checked as 2,000.
- **To target:** a points limit you set, like 2,000. The target is shown under the draft name, and Highlight errors flags any draft over it.

## Detachments {#detachments}

Some games, like Horus Heresy, build armies from **Detachments**. In these factions:

- New drafts start with any compulsory Detachments.
- Tap a Detachment to open it and add units to it. In the Draft view, its units are shown in a condensed list.
- Tap **+** in the Draft view to add more Detachments. To add a Detachment from another faction, such as an Allied Detachment, tap the faction name at the top of the Add list.
- To add extra Battlefield Roles, use **To** when adding units to a Detachment.
- Each Detachment can choose its own [traits](#traits). Until it does, it follows the draft's choice, shown as "same as" the draft.
- Tap the total to check your Detachment allowances. Expand each Detachment to see its own breakdown.
- If the faction has an **Allies** role, allied detachments count toward its limits.

There's a full walkthrough in [Using Horus Heresy Detachments]({{ '/post/793101846013165568/using-horus-heresy-detachments' | relative_url }}).

## Conditions and victory points {#conditions}

Conditions track what happens to units during a game, like a destroyed unit or a fighter in recovery. Every game system has a **Destroyed** condition. Systems such as Necromunda and Warhammer: The Old World include more detailed sets.

- **Set Conditions:** touch and hold a unit in the Draft view and choose **Conditions**. Or open the unit and tap the **Set Conditions** button (the heart) in the bottom toolbar.
- Conditions are shown as badges on the unit card. Destroyed units are dimmed and collapsed to reduce clutter.
- If the game system awards **victory points** for Conditions, tap the **Campaign** button (the trophy) in the Draft view to see the running total.
- To start a new game, tap **Campaign › Clear all Conditions**.

See [Conditions and Victory Points]({{ '/post/773086704386113536/conditions-and-victory-points' | relative_url }}) for a worked example.

## Experience points {#experience-points}

Track experience for campaign games.

- Open the unit and tap the **campaign tracking** button in the bottom-left corner to show the XP stepper. Once a unit has XP, the button shows its total instead.
- Tap the XP total for **Reset** and **Collapse**.
- In the Draft view, touch and hold a unit and tap **+1 XP** for a quick boost.

XP is shown in the top-right corner of the unit's card.

## Rules lookups {#lookups}

If the game system has a [website lookup](#advanced-game-and-faction-editing), you can look up rules on a rules site without leaving Quartermaster:

- Touch and hold a unit, option or special rule and choose **Lookup**.
- On iPad or Mac, use the **Lookup** menu in the menu bar to browse lookups for every unit in the draft.

## Publish a PDF or summary {#summarize-or-publish-a-draft}

In the Draft view, tap the **Publish** button in the toolbar:

- **Publish to PDF › as Roster Table** is a traditional table layout, best for event submissions and printing.
- **Publish to PDF › as Cards** shows each unit as a card, like the Draft view, which is best for quick reference during games.
- **View Summary** gives a short text summary with only the options you've chosen. It's perfect for forums, messages or sending to an event organiser.

In the PDF preview you can mark up, print, or share to other apps.

## Share a draft file {#share-a-draft}

To send the draft itself to another Quartermaster user, tap **Publish › Share Draft**, or tap the draft name and choose **Share Draft**. They need the same game system and faction installed to open it.

## Configure a faction {#configure-a-faction}

You can change any faction to fix a mistake, update points, or add new units.

- In the Faction view, tap the **faction name** at the top and choose **Configure Faction**. Or tap **Edit**, then **Configure Faction**.
- In the Library, swipe right on a faction and tap **Configure**.

In the configuration you can edit the faction's name, its units in each role, its armoury, and its **Traits & Composition**.

**Role limits:** tap the limits under a role's name to open its settings:

- **Lower limit** and **Upper limit** can each be a number of units or a percentage of the draft.
- **Per** makes number limits scale with the size of the game. For example, "0–2 per 1,000 points" allows 4 in a 2,000 point draft.
- **Unit Groups** limit how many of some units the role may have between them, like "0–3 of Knights or Questing Knights". Tap **Add unit group limit**, choose the units, and set the limits. They can scale per points too.

**Traits & Composition:** tap **Configure *faction* Traits & Composition** to set up [traits](#traits) and role comparisons:

- **Add Trait** creates a trait. Name it, turn on **Allow multiple variants** if drafts can choose several, then add variants. You can also copy traits from another faction, or paste variants one per line with **Generate from clipboard**.
- Tap a variant to edit it:
  - Give it a **theme** with base and trim colours.
  - Add **requirements**, shown as a reminder when choosing it.
  - Set a profile adjustment, like `M+1`, for units whose special rules include the trait in brackets, like `[Legion]`.
  - Add **Role Limits** that apply while it's chosen: a **percentage limit** caps how much of the army each unit in a role may be, and a **duplicate limit** caps how many units of the same name a role may have.
  - Turn on **Allow single restricted unit** to allow one unit the points would otherwise rule out, like a 0–1 per 1,000 points unit in a 750 point game.
- **Add Role Comparison** checks roles against each other. For example, "Elites and Fast Attack must be at most Troops", with optional multipliers and extra allowances.

To make a unit, option or item belong to a variant, put the variant's name in its requirements, like `Blood Angels`. To rule a variant out, put `!` in front, like `!Blood Angels`. The Add list and unit view then show or hide it to match each draft's choices.

> Changes to a faction don't update drafts you've already made. Add the unit again to pick up the new settings.

To configure a game system, tap its name in the Library to open it. Then tap the name at the top and choose **Configure Game System**, or tap **Edit**, then **Configure Game System**. There you'll find roles, default profiles, the armoury, Conditions, website lookups, and **Configure Traits** for traits every faction offers, like an allegiance. A faction's own trait with the same name takes the place of the game system's.

## Configure a unit {#configure-a-unit}

1. [Configure the faction](#configure-a-faction) that contains the unit.
2. Tap the unit in its role.
3. Change its name, size, points, profile, special rules, options and armoury allowances.

Each unit has a **size type**: single model, constant size, variable size, variable size with no maximum, grouping of multiple units, or detachment definition. The points can be set per model or per unit.

Options and armoury items can be added one at a time, copied from another unit, or pasted from text using **Generate from clipboard**.

## Create a game system or faction {#create-a-new-faction}

- **New game system:** in the Library, tap the **New Game System** button at the end of the Game Systems list.
- **New faction:** open the game system and tap the **New Faction** button at the end of the factions list. Choose **New Faction** to start from scratch, or **Copy from Faction** to start with the units and armoury of an existing faction.
- **Duplicate a faction:** in the Library, swipe right on the faction and tap **Duplicate**.

Once it's created, Quartermaster opens the configuration so you can start adding roles, units and items.

To delete a faction or game system, swipe left on it in the Library. You need to delete a faction's drafts first, and a game system's factions first.

## Share and contribute {#share-a-faction}

Open a game system or faction and tap the **Share** button in the bottom toolbar to send the file.

Made something others would enjoy? Email it to [{{ site.contribute_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contribute_email }}) to have it added to the template catalogue. Before you share an update, use **Set Version Number** in the configuration so people get notified about it.

## Tallies {#work-with-tallies}

Tallies count something other than points across a draft. For example:

- Power Rating in Warhammer 40,000
- the number of models with ranged weapons in The Lord of the Rings
- the total number of infantry, cavalry or vehicles in any game

**Using tallies:** tap the draft total to see each tally's total. Turn on **Show tallies** to show them on each unit's card.

**Adding a tally to a unit:**

1. [Configure the unit](#configure-a-unit) and tap **Advanced Configuration**.
2. Under **Tallies**, tap **Add Tally**.
3. Set the **name**. Tallies with the same name are added together across the draft. Tallies with different names are counted separately.
4. Set the value. Turn on **Multiply value by size** to count it per model rather than per unit.
5. Turn on **Only apply tally at specific sizes** if it should only count for some unit sizes.

For example, a unit with Power Rating 5 that goes up by 4 when expanded from 6 to 10 models needs two tallies. Give it one "Power Rating" tally of 5, and a second "Power Rating" tally of 4 that only applies at sizes 6 to 10.

To remove a tally, swipe left on it.

## Advanced settings {#advanced-game-and-faction-editing}

**Unit limits and requirements:** in a unit's **Advanced Configuration**, set a lower and upper limit for how many of the unit its role may have, and the requirements shown under its name. Limits can apply **per** so many points, like 0–1 per 1,000 points, and only **from** a certain size of game, like from 3,000 points.

**Points limit:** in **Advanced Configuration**, set the largest share of the army a unit may be, like 25%. Inside a detachment, it's a share of the detachment's points.

**Link requirement:** in **Advanced Configuration**, list the units, detachments or roles this unit or detachment must be [linked](#links) to, one per line. **Copy Previous** reuses the requirement from the unit above.

**Starting XP:** in **Advanced Configuration**, under **Campaign Settings**, set how much [experience](#experience-points) the unit starts with.

**How often an option can be equipped:** in an option's settings, **Equip** can be set to once only, the entire unit, up to a set maximum, up to the unit's size, up to a multiple of the unit's size, or the same as the previous option. With **up to set maximum**, you can also set a **Minimum**: the option starts already equipped that many times.

**Points multipliers:** in a unit's configuration, tap **Set multiplier** to add a multiplier that scales the unit's whole cost. Give it a name, choose how to round the result, and choose whether it's equipped by default.

**Detachment definitions:** set a unit's size type to **detachment definition** to make it a Detachment that other units are added into. Detachment definitions are marked with a flag icon in the faction's role list.

**Conditions:** in a game system's configuration, under **Unit Conditions**, tap **Add a set of Conditions**. Each Condition can shade the unit, show up separately in totals, and award victory points, either fixed or as a percentage of the unit's cost, to you or your opponent.

**Website lookup:** in a game system's configuration, enter a **Lookup URL** for a rules website. Use `%@` where the search term goes, for example `https://example.com/search?q=%@`.

**Version numbers:** tap **Set Version Number** in a game system or faction's configuration before you share an update. The in-app catalogue uses it to tell people an update is available.

## iCloud and storage {#icloud}

Quartermaster stores your game systems, factions and drafts in iCloud Drive, so they sync between your devices and are backed up automatically.

If your device is low on storage, iOS may offload files to iCloud. They show a cloud icon. Tap one to download it again. If files seem to be missing, tap **Refresh** at the bottom of the Library and give iCloud a moment to sync.

To keep your files on your device instead of in iCloud, see [Quartermaster and iCloud storage]({{ '/post/184238870659/quartermaster-and-icloud-storage' | relative_url }}).
