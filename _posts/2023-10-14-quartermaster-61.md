Quartermaster levels up editing powers!

## 🎲 Create Game Systems

Add your own custom Game System with the button at the end of the list of Game Systems and Factions. Send it to [contribute@quartermaster.app](mailto:contribute@quartermaster.app) to share!

## 🎛️ iPad Optimisations

Quartermaster has been reworked to make better use of the larger screen.

## 📑 Document Menus

Access document actions like Rename, Share, and Duplicate by tapping on the Draft title menu.

## 👆 Swipe Shortcuts

Swipe left or right on rows throughout the system for handy shortcuts.

**Available now in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/quartermaster/id879731050).**

Feeling lost or can’t find something? Get in touch at [support@quartermaster.app](mailto:support@quartermaster.app).
