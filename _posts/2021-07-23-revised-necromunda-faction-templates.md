All Necromunda faction templates have been updated with multiple improvements for running your gang in the app. It’s now much easier to manage XP, advancements, and lasting injuries for each fighter, and exotic beasts are split out to be managed separately. Support for the different types of campaign has been improved, and Outlaw Brutes have been added to the Hired Guns and Hangers-on template.
