Quartermaster adds quicker ways to navigate and maintain your templates.

## 🎲 In-app Template Catalogue

Browse, check for updates, and install popular templates without leaving Quartermaster.

## 🕰️ Recent Factions

The new row of shortcuts lets you quickly switch between your most active Factions.

## 🔭 Rules Lookups

Add search URLs for rules compendiums to Game Systems, allowing you to long press on rules to access Lookups.

**Available now in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/quartermaster/id879731050).**
