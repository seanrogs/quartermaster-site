## Improved iCloud management

When your device is low on space, iOS clears out files that it knows can safely be downloaded again from iCloud.

- Quartermaster now clearly indicates which files are in the cloud and which are on your device
- Tap a draft or faction with a cloud icon to download it straight away

## Other improvements

- Multiple speed improvements have been made to streamline the experience
- Items equipped by default can now be unequipped if required

## Bug fixes

- Fixed layout issues where content wasn’t visible or could not be scrolled to
- Fixed alphabetical ordering of drafts
- Fixed an issue where a unit profile could not be cleared
- Fixed intelligent naming of new drafts
