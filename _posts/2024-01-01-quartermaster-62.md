Quartermaster adds more ways to muster and review your forces.

## 🪪 Improved Profile Displays

Profiles are now displayed in a familiar table layout to improve readability. This extends to the fully redesigned Roster Table PDF. Check it out through the Share menu.

## 🧮 Adding Units and Detachments

Easily add units from other factions to your draft with the options at the top of the Add Unit view. Add to a secondary detachment or choose a different role from the usual.

## 📝 Personalised Equipment and Notes

Add custom details to units without making changes to the faction. Perfect for house rules or unique campaign rewards! Find the new options in the Personalise menu.

**Available now in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/quartermaster/id879731050).**
