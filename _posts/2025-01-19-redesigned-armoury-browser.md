The Armoury browser, used for adding Magic Items in games like Warhammer: The Old World, has been improved in Quartermaster 6.4.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087907995107328/1.png' | relative_url }})

When a unit has access to multiple armoury categories, the categories begin collapsed with a summary of assigned items on the right. The total points is still shown in the top right. Tap any category to expand it and view all the items.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087907995107328/2.png' | relative_url }})

Switch the Filter switch to only show armoury items that cost less than the unit’s remaining allowance. This is useful for filling up an armoury allowance. You can also type a number into the Search field to filter by a different maximum cost, which is great for spending those last few points for an army.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087907995107328/3.png' | relative_url }})

Searching for words still narrows the list to just those matches.

## Armoury items can now adjust unit profiles

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087907995107328/4.png' | relative_url }})

Armoury items can now include profiles, which can also include profile adjustments for the unit: values which are added or subtracted to the unit’s characteristics. Adjusted characteristics are shown in bold, the same as for unit upgrades that adjust characteristics. In this example, the Dwarf King has a combination or runes that boosts his WS, S, T, and W. He is mounted on Shieldbearers upgrade for a further boost to his Toughness!
