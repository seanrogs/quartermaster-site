## Dark Mode

Quartermaster has full support for iOS 13’s Dark Mode. No need to blind yourself when inspiration strikes in the middle of the night!

## Refreshed UI

Quartermaster’s look and feel has been refreshed in line with iOS 13 stylings. This includes better support for adjustable text sizes.
