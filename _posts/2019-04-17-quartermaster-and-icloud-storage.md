Quartermaster uses iCloud to store all your factions and drafts. This has a number of benefits:

- Easily access your files across multiple devices.
- Automatically backup to iCloud so you never lose your work.
- Save storage space on your device by leaving the files in the cloud until you need them.

However if you have limited storage space on your device you may need to constantly re-fetch your files from iCloud. This is because iOS has to share the limited space between other apps you use and knows it can safely offload Quartermaster files to make room. You’ll notice the same thing happening in the Music app, the Books app, and even with apps themselves that you don’t commonly use. The easiest way to stop needing to constantly re-fetch your files is to free up more storage space on your device. This will mean that iOS doesn’t need to aggressively free up your files that can be safely re-fetched from iCloud.

If you would prefer your files to be stored locally rather than use iCloud, follow the steps below.

## Turn off iCloud for Quartermaster

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Select your Apple ID account details at the top.
3. Select **iCloud** to view the iCloud settings.
4. Under the **Apps using iCloud** section, turn off the switch for Quartermaster.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/184238870659/1.png' | relative_url }})

- Quartermaster is now using your local device storage. This means anything you create in Quartermaster will no longer be available on other devices, and you’ll need to ensure your device is backed up or risk losing your work.
- If you had files saved in iCloud, these will no longer be available when you open Quartermaster. You’ll need to move existing files from iCloud to your local device storage.

## Move existing files from iCloud to your local device storage

After iCloud has been turned off, you’ll have no files in your local storage. We need to first add a file so we have a local location to which to move our existing iCloud files.

1. Download any game system in Quartermaster to initialise your local storage.
2. Open the **Files** app.
3. Select **iCloud Drive**, then select the Quartermaster folder. You’ll see all your existing files.
4. Tap the **Select** button and then tap the **Select All** button.
5. Tap the folder button in the bottom toolbar.
6. Expand **On My iPhone/iPad** and select the Quartermaster folder.
7. Tap the **Copy** button to copy your existing files from iCloud to local storage.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/184238870659/2.png' | relative_url }})

- Quartermaster will now have access to your files on local storage. You may need to quit Quartermaster by swiping it out of the app switcher and launch it again.
- When Quartermaster is using local storage there is a delay after launching the app before the files appear.
