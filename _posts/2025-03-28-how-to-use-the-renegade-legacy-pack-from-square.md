[Square Based has released the Renegade Legacy Pack](https://www.squarebased.com/), modifying the Legacy armies for Warhammer: The Old World. It’s fairly easy to adopt the changes from the pack in Quartermaster without needing specific changes to faction templates.

Quartermaster is a permissive army manager, while most army list managers are restrictive. This means that Quartermaster doesn’t force you to build your army in a specific way, with big red warnings where you don’t fulfil specific requirements. As such, there’s no problem picking different amounts of units than the minimums and maximums specified in the original Legacy documents.

Where units have moved to different roles, such as moving a unit from Rare to Special, Quartermaster caters for this too. In the “add units” view, tap the faction name at the top of the list to expand the options. You can then change the “Add units to” setting to the role to which you want the unit added.

Finally, for profile changes and new equipment options, Quartermaster allows you to add custom equipment to a unit. Tap the “Personalise” button in the bottom left of the unit view and select “Add custom equipment”. A special entry will appear in the equipment list; swipe the entry and tap the purple edit button. Here you can adjust all the custom equipment’s attributes. If you want to adjust the unit’s profile, specify the changes in the Profile field (for example, enter “Ld+1” to modify the unit’s Ld characteristic). When you’re done, swipe the editing sheet down. Finally, tap the custom equipment to equip it as normal.

Of course, if you want to change any of these on a more permanent basis, you can always configure your factions right within Quartermaster. In the faction view, tap the “Configure” button in the bottom left corner. From there, you can make permanent changes to listed requirements, unit roles, unit profiles and equipment.
