Quartermaster 6.7 lets your drafts tell more of your army's story.

## 🪄 Traits That Shape Your Army

Choose trait variants for your Draft and Detachments, and Units follow along: profiles, special rules, upgrades and the Add Unit list all adjust to match. Game systems can offer traits to every faction too. See [Traits]({{ '/user-guide#traits' | relative_url }}) in the User Guide.

## 🔗 Link Units and Detachments

Connect units and detachments that belong together, jump straight to what they're linked to, and spot missing links at a glance. Links appear in your published lists as well. See [Links]({{ '/user-guide#links' | relative_url }}).

## 🎨 Make It Yours with Themes

Give each Draft its own colours with custom themes, checked for contrast so everything stays easy to read in light and dark mode. See [Themes]({{ '/user-guide#themes' | relative_url }}).

## And more

- **Smarter composition checks:** role limits that scale with your points, unit group limits, role comparisons, and points limits for units and detachments. The new **Breakdown** button counts your errors and lists them all in one place.
- **Move units** to another role or detachment with **Change Role** and **Change Detachment**.
- **Simpler Add Unit settings:** choose the usual roles, a separate detachment, or a custom role from one picker.
- **Allies:** allied detachments count toward a faction's Allies role.
- **Sort drafts by trait** in the Faction view.
