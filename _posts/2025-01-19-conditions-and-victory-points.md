In Quartermaster 6.4, you can add Conditions to a unit to track in-game effects. This can be helpful in games like Necromunda, where fighters can suffer deaths or go into recovery, which need to be tracked as part of a campaign.

Additionally, Conditions can have Victory Points associated with them. This is great for tracking progress in games like Warhammer The Old World with a complex victory condition system.

All game systems can use a “Destroyed” condition, which dims the unit to reduce clutter while playing. [Necromunda](https://templates.quartermaster.app/GW.html#Necromunda) and [Warhammer: The Old World](https://templates.quartermaster.app/WHFB.html#TOW) have both been updated with a more in depth set of Conditions. Install the updated game systems (available from the in-app template browser) to see Conditions in action.

## Setting Conditions

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773086704386113536/1.png' | relative_url }})

From the Draft view, long press on any unit and select Set Conditions from the menu. Conditions are shown as badges on the unit card.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773086704386113536/2.png' | relative_url }})

A Destroyed or Dead unit appears collapsed and dimmed to remove visual clutter.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773086704386113536/3.png' | relative_url }})

You can also set Conditions from within the unit view. Tap the Conditions button underneath the unit profile.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773086704386113536/4.png' | relative_url }})

Tap any Condition badge to change the Conditions.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773086704386113536/5.png' | relative_url }})

If Conditions have associated Victory Point calculations, you can see the running total by tapping the Victory Points trophy button. Here you can see the total is 106 points for the dead Glad Riders shown earlier, plus 50 points for the Wild Riders’ banner having been claimed, plus 50 points (25% of the Wild Riders cost) for being reduced to below 25%.

You can clear Conditions for a unit from their individual Condition menus, or clear all Conditions for the whole draft from the Victory Points menu.
