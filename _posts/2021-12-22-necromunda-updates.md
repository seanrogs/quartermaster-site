All Necromunda files have been updated with content from The Book of the Outcast.

### New Underhive Outcasts Gang

- Choose from the Outcast Leader profiles, an Outcast Delegation, or an Elevated Leader
- Select from all the Delegation fighters listed in The Book of the Outcast
- Add an allied detachment to add any fighter as an Elevated Leader

### New Wyrd Powers

- The Necromunda game system has been updated with all the new Wyrd Powers. Download the new system to access the powers through Skills selections.

### Outlander Campaign

- Each faction has been given an Outlander Settlement in the *Other* section to track resources and settlement features.
