Quartermaster 6.5 improves Detachment-based Armies

## 🚩 Detachment Definitions

Factions can now include Detachment Definitions. This gives a new layer of hierarchy when drafting an army list.

Check the Catalog for updated Horus Heresy Factions with Detachments.

## 📇 New Detachment Presentation

Detachments (including existing Allied Detachments) are now summarized at the main Draft level. Tap on a Detachment to expand it and manage the units it contains. Check out [Using Horus Heresy Detachments](/post/793101846013165568/using-horus-heresy-detachments) for more.

## 🎨 UI Refresh

Quartermaster has been tuned and tweaked in preparation for the next generation of user interface design.
