Quartermaster levels up with game and campaign tracking.

## 🩺 Unit Conditions

Apply Conditions to a unit to keep track of in-game effects and victory points.

Check out [Conditions and Victory Points](/post/773086704386113536/conditions-and-victory-points) for more details.

## 🏋️ Experience Points

Manage XP for each unit to record campaign progress over multiple games.

See [Experience Points](/post/773087198974296064/experience-points) for an example.

## 👑 Improved Armoury Browser

Search for armoury items, filter by armoury categories, and limit the results to remaining points.

Read more about the [Redesigned Armoury Browser](/post/773087907995107328/redesigned-armoury-browser).

**Available now in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/quartermaster/id879731050).**
