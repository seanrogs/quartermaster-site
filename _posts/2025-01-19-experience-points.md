In Quartermaster 6.4, you can assign Experience Points to units. This is great for any game with a Campaign system, or even for keeping track of individual victory conditions in a one-off game.

## Adding XP

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087198974296064/1.png' | relative_url }})

In the unit view, if the XP stepper is not already shown, tap the Experience Points button on the right underneath the unit profile. If Experience Points have already been assigned, the button will show the XP total instead.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087198974296064/2.png' | relative_url }})

The XP stepper simply steps up and down the unit Experience Points. Tap the XP value itself to hide the stepper again.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/773087198974296064/3.png' | relative_url }})

Experience Points are shown prominently in the top right of a unit card on the draft view.
