Totally redesigned and rewritten, Quartermaster is easier to use and more powerful than ever.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/702753994040393728/1.png' | relative_url }})

The Library view organizes your Game Systems, Factions, and Drafts. Search across them all to find your work quickly. Recently used Drafts appear at the top of the Library so they’re always just a tap away.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/702753994040393728/2.png' | relative_url }})

The revised Draft view displays each unit as a card with all the details you need. Extra rules and profiles are available if you need them, or tap the collapse button to focus on just the essentials. The card view is also available when exporting for PDF or print, alongside an improved list layout.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/702753994040393728/3.png' | relative_url }})

There are lots of little improvements to listhammering. If the Faction template supports it, equipping specific options will adjust the unit profile, and you can see characteristic adjustments in bold. You can now add a unit from another Faction into your primary detachment: they’re no longer only confined to allied detachments.

![]({{ '/assets/posts/702753994040393728/4.png' | relative_url }})

Faction configuration and editing has also been rethought. There’s a lot of conveniences added that make it even more powerful when authoring your own templates. Just tap the gear button in the Faction view to configure the Faction or Game System.

Most importantly, Quartermaster 6 is a stronger foundation for the future. It doesn’t stop here! There’s more to come…
