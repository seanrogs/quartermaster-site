Quartermaster 6.7 tells more of your army's story.

## 🎨 Make It Yours With Themes

Give each Draft its own colours with custom themes, or shroud them in darkness. Quartermaster checks the contrast, so everything stays easy to read. Themes shine through in the Draft and Faction views, and in your summaries and published PDFs. See [Themes]({{ '/user-guide#themes' | relative_url }}) in the User Guide.

## ✅ Validate With More Tools

Connect units and detachments that belong together with Links, then jump straight to what they're linked to. Validate roles and units through percentages, minimums, maximums and duplicates, with limits that can scale with the size of your game. The new Breakdown button counts your errors and lists them all in one place. Publish Detachments as an Overview to see how it all fits together. See [Links]({{ '/user-guide#links' | relative_url }}) and [Highlight errors]({{ '/user-guide#highlight-errors' | relative_url }}).

## 🛡️ Shape Your Army With Traits

Choose traits for your Draft and Detachments, and units follow along: profiles, special rules, upgrades and the available units all adjust to match. Traits can carry their own themes and validation, and game systems can offer traits to every faction. See [Traits]({{ '/user-guide#traits' | relative_url }}).

## And more

- **Move units** to another role or detachment with Change Role and Change Detachment.
- **Refreshed publishing:** arrange card PDFs by role or as a stack, and share the text summary as an image, text or Markdown. Every PDF has a new header showing your faction, traits and points.
- **Campaign tracking:** see your draft's total XP and VP for you and your opponent, apply Conditions more than once, and offer different Conditions to different traits.
- **Simpler Add Unit settings:** choose the usual roles, a separate detachment or a custom role from one picker.
- **Allies:** allied detachments count toward a faction's Allies role.
- **Sort drafts by trait** in the Faction view.
