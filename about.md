As a wargamer, you're always thinking of new ways to put together an army, force or squadron for your favourite games. Quartermaster lets you draft those ideas whenever inspiration strikes. When it's time to play, all your miniatures are in order, with no more loose pages and scribbled notes.

Quartermaster uses game systems and faction templates made by a community of wargamers just like you. You can also create and edit your own factions right in the app.

## A permissive army manager

Most army list apps are *restrictive*: they force you to build an army one specific way, with big red warnings when you step outside the rules. Quartermaster is *permissive*. It shows your points, role limits and percentages, and it can highlight errors, but it never stops you building what you want. That makes it easy to use house rules, campaign supplements, community packs and brand-new editions on day one.

## Features

- Community-made game systems and factions, installed and updated in the app
- Unit cards with profiles, options, armoury items and special rules
- Points breakdowns, tallies, role percentages, targets and error highlighting
- Detachments, allies, and units from other factions
- Conditions, victory points and experience for games and campaigns
- Roster table and card PDFs, text summaries, and shareable draft files
- iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac
- Full editing of game systems and factions
